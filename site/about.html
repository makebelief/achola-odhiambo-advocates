About the firm

Practical counsel. Deliberate action.

Achola Odhiambo & Company Advocates is a Nairobi legal practice serving clients across dispute resolution, corporate, employment, property, family, constitutional and intellectual property matters.

01 · Positioning

A direct legal relationship built on preparation and clarity.

The practice combines contentious work with day-to-day legal advisory support.

The firm's approach is to identify the legal question, organise the facts and documents that matter, explain the available route, and move the matter forward deliberately.

Public court records show the firm appearing in Employment and Labour Relations Court, Environment and Land Court and Court of Appeal proceedings, supporting a litigation-led positioning alongside its wider advisory work.
