Privacy

Privacy Policy

Website enquiries

This is an informational website. The enquiry form prepares a message on your device and opens WhatsApp. It does not submit your message to a database operated by this website. Nothing is sent to the law firm until you choose to send it through WhatsApp.

WhatsApp is a third-party service and may process information according to its own privacy policy. Avoid including confidential details, case evidence, identity documents or sensitive information in an initial enquiry. Contacting the firm does not itself create an advocate-client relationship.

Hosting and third-party media

The hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses, device information and access logs to provide and protect the service. Images loaded from Pexels and Unsplash may cause your browser to connect to those providers. The site does not include advertising trackers or visitor analytics in its supplied source code.

Information handled by the firm

If you contact the firm by telephone or WhatsApp, the firm may use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry and, where appropriate, fulfil legal or professional obligations. Retention and access rights depend on the circumstances and applicable Kenyan law. For data enquiries, contact the firm using the telephone number on the contact page.
